Who We Are

A Legacy of Trust & Excellence

Founded in 2010, Horizon Properties began with a simple vision: to redefine the luxury real estate experience through transparency, expertise, and genuine care for every client's journey.

Today, we are a globally recognized advisory with presence in 38 international markets, having facilitated over $1.2 billion in property transactions. Our team of seasoned advisors brings unparalleled market intelligence and a commitment to personalized service.

We don't just sell properties — we help our clients find homes, build investments, and create lifestyles that reflect their highest aspirations.
